# V1.8 — Stage 2: hierarchical editorial taxonomy

## Scope and data-safety contract

This stage introduces a derived, three-level classification for poems and pinned texts. It does not rewrite poem bodies, titles, authors, IDs, source URLs, original sections, or original tags. Existing archive shard labels are retained as `archiveCategory` / `sourceCategory` provenance.

The first level is exactly:
- `مذهبی`
- `عمومی`

Every classified item receives:
- `category1`: first-level class
- `category2`: principal subject / person / theme
- `category3`: subtopic
- `taxonomyConfidence`: `high`, `medium`, or `low`
- `taxonomyReview`: empty when no review flag is required; otherwise a human-readable reason
- `taxonomyVersion`: `1.8.0`

## Classification policy

1. Explicit, distinctive religious names and subjects take precedence over general themes.
2. A bare ambiguous personal name is not sufficient to identify a sacred person. For example, the word «فاطمه» alone is not treated as proof that a poem concerns Lady Fatima.
3. Explicit legacy tags are normalized through a controlled alias table; source tags themselves remain unchanged.
4. When a shard has only a broad archive category, it is mapped to a matching broad second/third-level topic and flagged for editorial review.
5. When neither body nor tags support a reliable topic, the item is still placed under «عمومی / موضوعات متنوع / نامشخص» and flagged rather than silently guessed.
6. Pinned documents and historical texts are kept distinct from ordinary poem subjects.
7. Archive classification runs as each shard loads and is re-applied once the archive reports ready. Web-loaded Hafez records are classified on their load event.

## Initial taxonomy families

### Religious
- Named Ahl al-Bayt figures (separate person-level branches)
- Ahl al-Bayt as a collective subject
- Qur'an and Qur'anic teachings
- Supplication and munajat
- Pilgrimage and holy sites
- Religious occasions and rites
- Religious history and sira
- Beliefs and religious teachings
- Elegy and mourning

### General
- Love and union
- Separation and longing
- Loneliness and estrangement
- Hope and waiting
- Suffering and heartbreak
- Mysticism and spirituality
- Wisdom and ethics
- Social criticism
- Nature and description
- Death and passage of time
- Satire and humor
- Reflection and philosophy
- Narrative and story
- Epic and national themes
- Miscellaneous / multi-topic / unresolved

The third level is intentionally conservative in this implementation. It provides a stable subtopic label where the available text or tags justify one; it does not claim a human literary reading of every poem.

## Library and search integration

The library now exposes a separate «زیرموضوع‌ها» group alongside first-level classes, main subjects, and the existing archive-topic group. Selecting a third-level topic filters the loaded works by that derived field. The search filters continue to derive their third-level choices from the selected first- and second-level values.

## Archive integration

The archive loader now stamps each decoded record with its source shard name before adding it to the live collection. The taxonomy engine then derives the three-level path while retaining that provenance. This is important because a broad archive shard such as «طبیعت و توصیف» is not itself equivalent to the final editorial hierarchy.

## Review queue and known limitations

- Rule-based classification is an initial editorial pass, not a substitute for human review.
- Short poems, metaphor-heavy poems, multi-topic works, irony, allusion, and poems whose subject is implicit can be misclassified or flagged.
- A named figure can appear incidentally rather than as the poem's central subject; automatic rules cannot always resolve that distinction.
- Broad archive fallbacks and unresolved records remain marked for review.
- The external Ganjoor Hafez loader remains network/cache dependent; when its 200 records are loaded, they are classified by the same engine.
- The final review count must be measured against the full 56,880-record archive in the browser integration run. A local unit test alone does not establish full-archive coverage.

## Verification

Playwright regression coverage is added for:
- religious entity classification and three-level metadata;
- avoiding a false sacred-person match from an ambiguous bare name;
- preserving source shard provenance and surfacing broad-shard review flags;
- full archive coverage and absence of missing hierarchy fields after archive boot.

Stage 2 is considered technically applied when the full archive integration test passes. It is not considered editorially human-reviewed until the flagged review queue has been inspected and adjudicated.
